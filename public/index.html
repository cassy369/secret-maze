The idea behind the game

SECRET MAZE was created as a classic maze experience for people who enjoy short, skill-based browser games. The goal is simple: enter the maze, learn its layout, make smart moves and reach the exit.

Later stages add more pressure through tighter routes, hazards, secrets and time limits. The game is intended to be playable on both desktop and mobile devices.
